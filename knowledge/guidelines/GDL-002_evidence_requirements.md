# GDL-002: Fraud Evidence Requirements and Burden of Proof Standards

> **DISCLAIMER**: This document is synthetic demonstration data generated solely for the FraudDNA project buildathon. It does not represent real Razorpay evidentiary standards or legal frameworks.

## 1. Objective
To maintain high precision and minimize costly false positives, FraudDNA enforces a strict multi-pillar standard of proof before any restrictive policy recommendation can be validated.

## 2. Multi-Pillar Evidence Hierarchy

### Pillar 1: Statistical ML Risk
- **Standard**: Probability of fraud generated by the supervised LightGBM model.
- **Operating Threshold**: Classification threshold is set at 0.37 on held-out validation.
- **Requirement**: ML prediction alone is NOT sufficient to classify an entire network as a syndicate. A single elevated score only marks the individual transaction as an anomaly.

### Pillar 2: Explainable Attribution (Tree SHAP)
- **Standard**: Verifiable feature attributions showing which specific behavioral or entity signals drove the risk score.
- **Requirement**: The top 3 positive risk drivers must correlate with the observed behavioral pattern (e.g. elevated entity velocity, night timing, high amount ratio).

### Pillar 3: Graph Relationship Evidence
- **Standard**: Verified structural connectivity in the FraudDNA NetworkX graph.
- **Requirement**: Coordinated abuse claims require verifiable multi-customer sharing of bridge entities (hardware devices, IP addresses, or payment cards).

### Pillar 4: Cluster Risk & Isolation
- **Standard**: Connected component evaluation excluding high-degree merchant bridge nodes.
- **Requirement**: Suspicious cluster determination requires `cluster_risk_score >= 0.50`, presence of relationship collusion (`SHARED_DEVICE_COLLUSION`, `IP_PROXY_FARM`, or `CARD_CYCLING`), and at least 2 member transactions.

## 3. Prohibited Determinations
- Never infer coordination solely from high transaction amount.
- Never mark a giant legitimate component as suspicious merely because it contains isolated anomalies.
- Never cite ungrounded or fabricated policy clauses.
