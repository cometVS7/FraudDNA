# FraudDNA V2 — Product Vision

## Product Identity

FraudDNA V2 is an AI-native fraud intelligence and financial crime operations platform.

It is not simply a fraud prediction dashboard.

Its purpose is to help financial organizations detect, understand, investigate, simulate, and govern complex fraud and abuse operations.

## Core Product Question

V1 asks:

"Is this transaction suspicious?"

V2 asks:

"Why is this activity risky, what entities and transactions is it connected to, what attack pattern may be occurring, what evidence supports that conclusion, what should an investigator do next, and how confident are we?"

## Product Pillars

### 1. Transaction Intelligence

Understand individual transaction behavior and assign calibrated risk.

### 2. Entity Intelligence

Build behavioral profiles around customers, devices, IPs, cards, merchants, accounts, and other relevant entities.

### 3. Network Intelligence

Discover relationships and coordinated fraud operations that individual transaction scoring cannot see.

### 4. Investigation Intelligence

Give investigators a complete evidence-driven workspace for understanding suspicious activity.

### 5. AI Investigation

Use bounded AI agents to gather evidence, retrieve relevant intelligence, formulate hypotheses, and produce structured investigation findings.

AI must not have unrestricted authority over financial actions.

### 6. Decision Intelligence

Translate model and investigation evidence into deterministic, auditable policy outcomes.

### 7. Simulation & Strategy

Allow risk teams to test thresholds, policies, operational capacity, and financial tradeoffs before changing production controls.

### 8. Model Intelligence

Measure model performance, calibration, drift, feature behavior, false positives, false negatives, and financial impact.

### 9. Governance & Audit

Maintain an explainable, tamper-evident record of risk signals, investigations, policies, decisions, and system actions.

## V2 Product Experience

The interface should feel like an operational system used by a serious fraud, risk, or financial-crime team.

It should not feel like:

- a student project
- a hackathon demo
- an AI toy
- a generic analytics dashboard
- a collection of disconnected charts

It should feel like:

- a fraud intelligence workstation
- an investigation operating system
- a financial risk command center
- an evidence-driven decision platform

## Design Principles

1. Information density over decoration.
2. Evidence over marketing.
3. Explainability over black-box behavior.
4. Real data over visual simulation.
5. Clear hierarchy over excessive UI elements.
6. Fast investigation workflows.
7. Every important number must have a traceable source.
8. Every AI output must be grounded and bounded.
9. Financial decisions remain deterministic and auditable.
10. The interface should communicate confidence, uncertainty, relationships, and evidence clearly.

## Long-Term Vision

FraudDNA should evolve from a transaction fraud detector into an intelligence layer that sits between payment activity and risk operations.

The platform should eventually support:

Transaction → Entity → Relationship → Pattern → Investigation → Evidence → Decision → Outcome → Learning

This loop should become the foundation of the product.

## V2 Success Definition

FraudDNA V2 is successful when a technically sophisticated user can:

1. identify abnormal activity,
2. understand why it is abnormal,
3. discover connected entities,
4. identify coordinated behavior,
5. investigate the underlying pattern,
6. retrieve supporting intelligence,
7. understand the AI's reasoning,
8. evaluate possible decisions,
9. understand financial and operational consequences,
10. audit exactly how the final decision was produced.

The goal is not maximum AI autonomy.

The goal is maximum useful intelligence with controlled, explainable, auditable automation.
